# Olympiad formats: what to copy

Researched October 2026. Rules change; check the current official documents before relying on a detail.

## IPhO (International Physics Olympiad)

- Theory: three problems, five hours in total; experiment: one or two problems, five hours; at least one full day
  of rest between them. Marks: 30 for theory, 20 for experiment; the organiser allocates marks inside each exam.
- Theory problems draw on at least four areas of secondary-school physics and must be solvable with standard
  high-school mathematics, without extensive numerical calculation.
- Syllabus principles (appendix to the statutes): problems test creativity and understanding of physics, not
  mathematical virtuosity or speed; the share of marks for mathematical manipulation is kept small; approximate
  solutions to hard mathematics earn partial credit; problem texts are concise.
- Concepts outside the syllabus are allowed if the text gives enough information for a student without prior
  knowledge, if they are closely related to syllabus topics, and if they are explained in terms of syllabus topics.
- Syllabus areas: mechanics; electromagnetic fields; oscillations and waves; relativity; quantum physics;
  thermodynamics and statistical physics. Mathematics: algebra, trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic
  functions, vectors, complex numbers, basic statistics, calculus, approximations (Taylor expansion, trapezoidal
  rule).
- Research topics appear routinely. Example: IPhO 2016 (Zurich) set a theory problem on the physics of the Large
  Hadron Collider, next to a mechanics problem and a nonlinear-circuit (thyristor) problem.

## APhO (Asian Physics Olympiad)

- Modelled on IPhO and run under similar statutes (accepted 2000; revised 2002, 2008, 2014). Three theoretical
  problems and one or two experimental problems, normally five hours each, on separate days with a rest day.
- Marks: 30 theory, 20 experiment; the organiser sets the marks of each problem.
- Theory problems involve at least four areas of high-school physics and use standard high-school mathematics.
- Problems and solutions are prepared in English by the host; leaders translate them for their students.
  Teams have up to eight students.
- Awards relative to a reference score (the smaller of the mean of the top three and twice the median): 90% first
  prize, 78% second, 65% third, 50% honourable mention.
- Layout seen in recent booklets (APhO 2025): page codes such as "T1-3", labelled parts with point totals, each
  subquestion in a box with its points, a context paragraph and given constants before the questions.

## PanPhO (Pan Pearl River Delta Physics Olympiad and Chinese Elite Schools Invitational)

- Organised by the Department of Physics, HKUST (sponsored by HKUST IAS); papers set and marked by HKUST.
  Participants: students from Hong Kong, Macau and invited mainland schools.
- Two tests. **Basic test (基础试)**: mechanics only, for junior and first-year senior students; the 2025 paper had
  16 multiple-choice and 2 short-answer questions for 100 marks, as reported by a secondary source; the official
  2026 notice gives 9:30-11:30.
  **Comprehensive test (综合试)**: all senior students; in 2026 two sessions, 9:30-11:00 and 13:30-17:00. Its
  problems are described as close to IPhO level and touching recent developments.
- The PanPhO outline combines the mainland syllabus with the IPhO syllabus and lists, under applications,
  "近代和现代重大成果，科研最新进展" (major modern achievements and latest research progress).
- Past papers and reference answers are on the official site.

## What this skill copies

| Feature | From | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 points per problem, 3-5 parts, boxed subquestions with points | IPhO/APhO | default size and layout |
| Out-of-syllabus concepts explained in syllabus terms | IPhO syllabus | Background, Toolkit, Glossary; the cold read |
| Small share of marks for algebra; partial credit for approximations | IPhO syllabus | marking breakdowns |
| Context paragraph and given data before each question | APhO/IPhO | green "AVAILABLE" frames |
| Frontier topics in a comprehensive paper | PanPhO, IPhO | research papers as the problem source |
| Short multiple-choice checks | PanPhO basic test | Lite mode vocabulary checks |
| English master text, translated by leaders | APhO | write once; keep symbols identical in translations |
